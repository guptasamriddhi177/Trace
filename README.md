TRACE is a claim investigation platform designed to help people understand what they see, read, and hear online. In a world where misinformation, misleading headlines, manipulated media, and AI-generated content spread rapidly, TRACE turns questionable claims into structured investigations.

Instead of simply giving a True or False answer, TRACE examines the claim, explores available sources, compares evidence, identifies contradictions, and uncovers missing context. It helps users understand not just what a claim says, but why it may or may not be trustworthy.
